About This Platform

This platform was created as part of INNOVIT 2026 by Team CodeBunnies to address a critical problem faced by Indian students: the lack of a centralized, transparent platform to compare education loan options.

The Problem

  • Students visit multiple banks individually
  • Loan information is scattered and confusing
  • Heavy dependency on agents and middlemen
  • Lack of transparency in eligibility criteria

Our Solution

  • One form to compare multiple banks
  • Clear, student-friendly comparison
  • No agents, completely free service
  • Transparent eligibility mapping

🇮🇳 Contributing to Atmanirbhar Bharat

By empowering students with financial clarity and reducing dependency on intermediaries, we promote self-reliant decision-making and support India's vision of an educated, self-sufficient workforce.
